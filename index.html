Research

My PhD sits at the intersection of physics-informed constraints and adversarial robustness. Do the constraints buy robustness for free, or do they open new attack surfaces? Where the two objectives conflict, what does the trade-off curve actually look like?

My previous research and work has been in privacy preserving ML techniques, especially homomorphic encryption.

I also keep an eye on the geometry of representations in large language models as well as AI safety topics.

Publications

  • R. Pazzi, A. Falcetta, M. Roveri. HEDEL: Homomorphically Encrypted Distributed Ensemble Learning. IJCNN, 2025.

    Combines distributed multi-key homomorphic encryption with transfer learning to merge models trained by separate parties. In settings like healthcare it can stand in for federated learning, cutting communication between parties and tightening the security guarantee.
